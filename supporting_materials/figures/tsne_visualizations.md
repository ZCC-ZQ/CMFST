# t-SNE Visualizations for NCMMSC and ADReSSo

Additional t-SNE visualizations of learned representations, retained from the original manuscript figures.

![NCMMSC learned representations](tsne_ncmmsc.png)

## (b) ADReSSo

![ADReSSo learned representations](tsne_adresso.png)

AD denotes Alzheimer's disease, HC denotes healthy/cognitively normal controls, and MCI denotes mild cognitive impairment. The HC label in the ADReSSo image corresponds to CN in the revised manuscript. These projections illustrate representation structure; they are not evidence of clinical biomarkers or a quantitative generalization test. The original raster images are retained unchanged. The exact historical t-SNE generation parameters have not been recovered; no parameters from other plotting versions are assigned to these images.
